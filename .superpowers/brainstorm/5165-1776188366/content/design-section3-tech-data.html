Flow Particles — How They Work in 3D

RENDERING

InstancedMesh — single draw call for all particles across all edges. Each instance: a small billboard quad with additive blending.

Shader trail — vertex shader stretches quad along velocity. Fragment shader applies gradient fade for comet tail. Trail length scales with speed.

BEHAVIOR PER EDGE TYPE

Contractual — slow, 2-3 particles, teal, steady flow
Risk — medium, 4-5, colored by severity
Cascade — fast, 6-8, purple, urgent pulse
Pattern — slow, 1-2, amber, dotted path

POST-PROCESSING

Bloom — makes particles glow beyond their geometry. Low threshold, high intensity on particle layer.
DOF — background nodes blur on Focus camera. Particles stay sharp.
Vignette — subtle darkening at edges for cinematic feel.
