Design Section 2 — Camera System & Interaction

3D camera presets, expand-in-place behavior, and the demo flow

Camera Presets

Three guided views the camera smoothly transitions between. No free orbit — the camera always moves to a preset.

Overview

Elevated ~45° angle. Sees the full network. Slow auto-rotation (~0.1°/s). Default landing view.

Command Table

Low angle, almost flat — like looking across a strategy table. Labels float above nodes. Best for comparing contractors side-by-side.

Focus

Camera flies to clicked node. Node expands, its satellites spread out. Background nodes blur + dim. Triggered by click.

Expand-in-Place Interaction

When a contractor node is clicked, the camera flies to it and the node "opens up" to reveal detail.

Resting state

Expanded state

Animation sequence (~800ms):
1. Camera starts easing toward the node (300ms)
2. Background nodes fade + blur (200ms, concurrent)
3. Clicked node scales up, satellites spread outward revealing labels (400ms, spring easing)
4. Expanded node shows richer detail — summary stats, severity arc, count badges

Dismissal: Click empty space or press Escape → reverse animation, camera returns to previous preset.

Nesting: From an expanded contractor, clicking a satellite (e.g., EW-0042) does a second-level focus — camera zooms further, satellite expands, and the AI thread panel slides in from the right edge as an HTML overlay.

Demo Flow (Guided Narrative)

1 — LAND

Overview camera. Full constellation. Auto-rotate. Headline number pulses in hub. "£93.2M exposure across 5 contractors"

2 — SPOT

Audience sees Afcons is largest + reddest node with most connections. Click it → Focus camera. Satellites reveal 3 EWs, 14 NCEs. "25% variation — 2x the average"

3 — CONNECT

Dashed cascade line from EW-0042 → EW-0058. Click it → see the £24M cascade path. "A 6-week delay becomes 14 weeks and £24M"

4 — CONTRAST

Escape back to overview. Click L&T — clean, green, sparse satellites. "This is what a well-managed contract looks like"
