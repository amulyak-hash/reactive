Pulse Line

Vertical line fixed to the right edge. A glowing dot crawls down as you scroll. Beat labels appear beside it. The line gradient shifts color per act (red → purple → blue). Minimal, elegant.

Breathing Dots

5 dots stacked vertically on the right edge. The active beat's dot is larger and glowing. Past beats are filled but dim. Future beats are hollow outlines. The active dot breathes (scale pulse). No labels — pure visual.

Story Thread

Connected nodes forming a thread. The active node is larger + glowing, with a contextual label that pops out ("Where the money went", "It's accelerating"). Past nodes are filled, future are hollow. The label changes per beat — it's a narrative breadcrumb.

Horizon Bar

Thin horizontal bar fixed to the top of the story area. Gradient fills left-to-right as you scroll (red → purple → blue). Tick marks for each beat. A glowing dot shows exact position. Current beat name below. Think Netflix episode progress.
