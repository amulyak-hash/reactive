The Story Spine

After the cold open (£1 → 13p), what beats pull the user through? Here's one possible arc — tell me what to add, remove, or reorder.

1
Act I • The Hook
£1 contracted → 13p leaked
The ratio, full screen. No explanation. A pause. Then a single line fades in beneath: "Across 5 contractors. In 7 months."
FEELING: alarm. "That's a lot."
2
Act II begins • The Fracture
"But the bleeding isn't equal."
Five contractor names appear. Each with their variation: 6%, 9.8%, 13.3%, 16%... and then 25%. Afcons glows. The others dim. The story narrows its focus.
FEELING: suspense. "One of these is not like the others."
3
Act II • The Villain Revealed
Afcons Infra: £35.5M on a £142M contract
Zooms into Afcons. The £35.5M breaks apart into its causes — ground conditions (£15.2M), design changes (£10.8M), access delays (£6.1M). Not abstract categories. Concrete money trails.
FEELING: suspense deepens. "Where did it all go?"
4
Act II • The Twist
"And it's accelerating."
The 7-month trajectory animates. Not a chart — a visual pulse, like a heartbeat monitor speeding up. Month over month, the bleed rate increases. The curve bends upward. NCC is the second story — quiet contractor, but trending fast.
FEELING: urgency. "This isn't slowing down."
5
Act III • Clarity
The benchmark exists: L&T at 6%
The resolution. L&T is managing the same project conditions at 6% variation. That's the proof that 25% isn't inevitable — it's a management failure. The story closes with a clear framing: what "good" looks like, who needs intervention, and what the next action is.
FEELING: clarity. "Now I know exactly what to do."
How it maps to the hybrid delivery
Beats 1-2 = controlled sequence (click/auto-advance). Tight pacing, full-screen reveals.
Beats 3-5 = scrollable deep-dive. User controls pace. Each beat is a "chapter" they scroll into.
