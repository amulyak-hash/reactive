Weighted Streams

Money trails. Each cause is a stream — wider = more money. They animate in one by one, largest first. No axes. The visual weight is the data. Glowing dots pulse by severity.

Typographic Scale

Numbers ARE the visualization. Bigger money = bigger type. Each figure counts up from zero. Short prose captions give the "why" — not a label, a micro-story. "The soil lied." "Scope kept moving."

Gravity Wells

Abstract spatial layout. The total is a central mass. Causes orbit around it — sized by value, colored by severity. They drift in with physics-based motion. Beautiful but more abstract — less immediately readable.

Or mix them
These aren't mutually exclusive. Typographic scale for the cold open numbers + weighted streams for the breakdown chapters could work well together. Tell me what resonates.
