How does the story unfold?

The narrative arc is: Alarm → Suspense → Clarity. But how does the user physically move through it?

A

The Scroll

One continuous vertical experience. The story reveals itself as you scroll down — each section builds on the last. Think longform journalism (NYT Snowfall, Bloomberg visual stories). Scroll-triggered animations bring data to life at the right moment.

Feels like
Reading a story. Cinematic. Each scroll reveals a new beat. No decisions required — just absorb.
B

The Sequence

A step-by-step progression — like slides or a briefing deck. Each "scene" fills the card. You advance with a click or gesture. The pacing is controlled — one revelation per beat. Think Apple keynote meets intelligence briefing.

Feels like
A briefing. Punchy. Each click is a reveal. The user controls pace but the story controls sequence.
C

The Hybrid

Starts as a sequence (the alarm hook — one or two dramatic scenes), then opens into a scrollable deep-dive for the suspense and clarity acts. The hook is tight and controlled; the exploration is freeform.

Feels like
A cold open that grabs you, then the investigation unfolds naturally. Best of both worlds but more complex to build.
